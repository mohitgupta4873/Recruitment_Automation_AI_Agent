Workflow

A 5-Step Automated Pipeline

Replace hours of manual work with one intelligent agent that handles everything from start to finish.

✍️
Smart JD Draft
Gemini AI generates a professional job description from your role title and experience requirements — or write your own any time.
🚀
Launch Campaign
Publishes your own hosted application page instantly. Optionally also creates a Google Sheet or posts to LinkedIn, if you've connected them.
🧠
AI Scoring
Candidates apply directly on your page and upload a resume — each one is parsed and scored against the role automatically, no manual sync step.
📅
Auto Schedule
Sends personalized calendar invites by email — through your own email provider by default, or your connected Gmail if you'd rather send as yourself.
⚖️
Hire or Reject
Bulk send offers or rejection emails, with decisions optionally logged to a Google Sheet if you've connected one.
Features

Everything You Need to Hire

A complete AI-powered toolkit for recruiters. Google and LinkedIn are optional bonuses layered on top — nothing here requires them.

🤖
Gemini AI JDs
Generate a structured, professional job description in seconds — or write your own any time, including if Gemini is ever unavailable.
🔗
Self-Hosted Apply Page
Every campaign gets its own public application page the moment you launch it — no Google Form, no third-party account needed for candidates to apply.
🔍
Resume AI Scoring
Candidates upload their resume directly; it's parsed and scored against role-specific keywords the moment they apply.
📧
Email Automation
Sends calendar invites, offers, and rejections automatically through your own configured email provider — or your connected Gmail, if you prefer sending as yourself.
📊
Google Sheets Tracking (optional)
Connect your Google account and every applicant, score, and decision is also logged to a dedicated Sheet automatically.
💼
LinkedIn Cross-Post (optional)
Connect LinkedIn and optionally post a campaign's job description straight to your own feed the moment you launch it.
