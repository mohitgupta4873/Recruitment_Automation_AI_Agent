Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

Draft, not yet legal-reviewed. This page is written to be accurate about what the application actually does today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should be before HireAI is used to process real candidates' data at any scale, particularly for hiring in the EU or New York City (see "Automated scoring" below).

Who this covers

HireAI is a hiring-automation tool: a recruiter ("the campaign owner") creates a campaign for a role, and candidates apply through that campaign's own public application page. Two different relationships fall under this policy:

  • Recruiter accounts. If you have a HireAI login, we are the controller of your account data (username, email, password hash, and — if you connect it — your Google OAuth credentials).
  • Candidate applications. If you applied to a role through a HireAI-hosted apply page, the campaign owner (the recruiter who created that campaign) is the controller of your application data — they decide who to interview and hire. HireAI acts as their processor: we host the infrastructure that stores, scores, and lets them act on your application, but we do not decide how it's used.

What we collect

  • From recruiters: account details (username, email, password — stored as a salted hash, never in plain text); if Google is connected, an encrypted OAuth credential used only to send email as you and export a tracking spreadsheet, both actions you trigger.
  • From candidates: full name, email, years of experience, a short free-text pitch, an optional LinkedIn URL, and the resume file itself. The resume is parsed to extract text, which is scored against a list of keywords the recruiter's job description implies — a resume is never sent anywhere outside this app for scoring beyond the AI call that turns the job description into that keyword list.

Cookies

HireAI sets exactly two cookies, both strictly necessary for the site to function and neither used for tracking or advertising: a session cookie (keeps you signed in) and a CSRF cookie (protects form submissions from cross-site forgery). No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies are set. Because both cookies are strictly necessary, no separate cookie-consent banner is shown — this section is that disclosure.

How long we keep it

Recruiter account data is kept for as long as the account exists — see "Deleting your account" below for how to remove it entirely.

Candidate resumes and the extracted text are kept while a campaign is open, and for a limited period after the recruiter closes it (the campaign's retention window, configurable per campaign, defaulting to 180 days) — after which the resume file and extracted text are automatically and permanently deleted. The rest of the application record (name, email, score, outcome) is retained for the recruiter's own hiring records unless a deletion request is made.

Your rights, and how to exercise them

You can ask for your application data to be deleted from a specific campaign at any time — email {{ privacy_contact_email }} with the campaign and the email address you applied with, and we will remove the resume file and application record. Recruiters can also action this directly for campaigns they own. If you have a HireAI account yourself, you can delete it — and everything tied to it — from your dashboard; see "Deleting your account" below.

Deleting your account

Recruiters can permanently delete their HireAI account from the dashboard. This immediately: disconnects and revokes any connected Google account, deletes every campaign you own along with every candidate application and resume file inside those campaigns, and removes your login. This action cannot be undone.

Automated scoring

Every candidate's resume is scored automatically against a keyword list derived from the role's job description. This score is advisory only — it is a starting point for a human recruiter's own review, never an automatic accept or reject, and HireAI does not make hiring decisions. Recruiters using this tool to hire in jurisdictions with automated-employment-decision-tool requirements (for example, New York City Local Law 144, or the EU AI Act's high-risk classification for employment AI) are responsible for their own compliance with those rules, including any required bias audit or candidate notice beyond what this page provides.

Third parties

We use Google's Gemini API to draft job descriptions and derive scoring keywords from them — no candidate resume content is sent to this API. If a recruiter connects their own Google account, invite emails and tracking-sheet exports are sent through their Google account under their own Google terms, not ours. If Google isn't connected, email is sent through our own transactional email provider instead.
