What HireAI is

HireAI is a hiring-automation tool. A recruiter account lets you draft a job description, launch a public application page for it, receive and automatically score candidate resumes, schedule interviews, and send offer or rejection emails. See the Privacy Policy for how data moves through that process.

Recruiter accounts

  • You're responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for all activity under your account.
  • You're responsible for the accuracy of job descriptions you publish and for how you use candidate data collected through your campaigns — HireAI is a processor of that data on your behalf, not the decision-maker (see Privacy Policy, "Who this covers").
  • You may not use HireAI to collect candidate data for a purpose other than genuine hiring, or to send bulk email to anyone who did not apply through your own campaign.
  • If you connect a Google account, you're granting HireAI narrow, revocable permission to send email and export a spreadsheet as you — revocable at any time from your dashboard or from your Google account's own permissions page.

Candidates

Applying through a HireAI-hosted apply page means your application details and resume are stored and automatically scored, as described on that apply page and in the Privacy Policy. The recruiter who owns that campaign — not HireAI — makes the actual hiring decision; the automated score is advisory only.

Acceptable use

Don't use HireAI to upload malicious files, attempt to access another user's campaigns or candidates, circumvent rate limits, or use the platform to send unsolicited email. Accounts found doing so may be suspended or deleted.
